# Example: fictional 8 GB setup

> Fully fictional device and results.

**Device:** 8 GB class iPhone, about 40 GB free.
**Goal:** Private chat plus light note Q&A, offline first. Abliterated variants acceptable.

## Recommendations (illustrative)

1. Runtime: open-source multi-backend app with MLX + llama.cpp and a fit check.
2. Daily driver: 2B to 4B Q4 instruct model from a current small Qwen or Gemma line.
3. Optional second: distilled reasoning quant only if the first stays cool under a five-question eval.

## Fictional benchmark notes

- 4B Q4: usable chat speed, warm but stable on a ten-minute session while plugged in
- 8B Q4: higher quality on two questions, then thermal throttle and a background kill once another app opened

## Outcome

Kept the 4B daily driver. Deferred graph layer to week two. Scheduled a weekly check for new on-device quants.
