# Attribution

This proof-of-work repository documents an on-device open-weight LLM advisor design by Thiago De Sa (2026).

## Design influences (ideas, not code)

- Graph engineering / GraphRAG style structure for retrieval and grounding on small devices.
- Super-agent harness ideas: router/worker split, inline-and-spill context, chain of recall, adapted to phone RAM and Shortcuts triggers.
- Community reports from public forums and catalogs (for example r/LocalLLM, r/LocalLLaMA, Hugging Face model cards, Pocket AI Lab, Private LLM catalogs, Swiftlet-class experimental runtimes). Specific tok/s and fit claims must be re-checked against current device reports before use.

None of the above are copied as proprietary text or code.
